UAply shows you one job at a time. Swipe right (or tap Apply) to apply, swipe left (or tap Pass) to skip. Set up your profile once, and every card tells you how well the job fits it before you decide.

REAL JOBS, FROM THE SOURCE
The listings come from employers' own public hiring systems and career pages (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters and Workable), federal jobs from USAJOBS, and well-known internship lists. Each card says where the job came from. Postings older than 60 days are left out, and the same job listed twice is shown once. Full-time jobs, part-time work, internships and co-ops each have their own list.

WHAT A RIGHT SWIPE DOES
- Where the employer's hiring system takes applications, UAply fills in that employer's real application form from your profile and sends it. If the form asks something your profile does not answer yet, UAply asks you right there.
- Where a job posting asks for applications by email, UAply emails your application and resume to that address.
- Everywhere else, the job is saved in your Job Tracker with a receipt, and the employer's own form opens in the app, already filled in from your profile, for you to check and submit.
- You can undo a swipe right after you make it. A left swipe only hides the job.

A MATCH SCORE YOU CAN READ
The match percentage is a fixed formula, not a judgment about you: skills, experience, pay, job title, work style, location and education, with the points for each part listed on the card. It only orders jobs for you. It never rejects you and never makes a hiring decision.

YOUR PROFILE, ONCE
Five short steps: about you, the jobs you want, location and pay, background and links, and eligibility. Attach your resume once and it goes with the applications UAply sends. Voluntary self-identification answers stay on your device and are never sent for you.

JOB TRACKER
Every application in one place: move it to Interview, Accepted or Rejected, add an interview time, an offer or notes, get a reminder before an interview, and export everything as a spreadsheet or calendar file with the iPhone share sheet.

FILTERS THAT MATTER
Job type, how recently it was posted, work style (remote, hybrid or on-site), commute distance, minimum match, and target titles. Your device location, if you choose to use it, stays on your device.

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
The UAply Assistant answers questions from UAply's help articles, and Talk to a person reaches the team.

FREE DURING EARLY ACCESS
Nothing is charged, there is no weekly limit on applications, and there are no ads.

PRIVACY
UAply does not sell your information or use it for advertising. Your profile and swipes are saved on your device, and an application's details go to an employer only when you apply to that employer. You get a receipt for each application and can delete your account at any time in Profile, Account. Privacy Policy: https://uaply.net/legal/privacy.html

UAply is for people 18 and older. It is the same service as uaply.net: sign in with the same account on both.
