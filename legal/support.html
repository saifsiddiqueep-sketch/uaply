Support

Last updated October 17, 2026.

How to reach us

Email uaplytechnologies@uaply.net. A person on the UAply team reads every message. We usually reply within 2 business days (Monday to Friday, US Eastern time).

In the app you can also tap Help, then Talk to a person. Your message comes to the same inbox, and our reply goes to the email you give.

What to include

  • The email address you use for UAply (if you have an account).
  • What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • For a job: the company and the job title as they appear on the card.
  • Your device and browser (for example "iPhone 15, Safari" or "Windows laptop, Chrome").
  • A screenshot, if it helps.

Never send your password, a verification code, or your Social Security or bank details. We will never ask for them.

For employers

Questions about Hiring mode (your company profile, job posts or applicants), or a request to correct or remove a listing that shows your company: email the same address and say which company and job it is about. Posting jobs is free while we are in early access.

Common requests

  • Delete my account or data: you can do it yourself in the app (Profile, Account, More account options). See also the Privacy Policy.
  • I forgot my password: on the Sign in screen, tap Forgot password? and we email you a link that works for one hour.
  • Sign-in or buttons missing with Brave Shields or another blocker: UAply uses one sign-in cookie and stores your profile on your device; it has no ads or trackers. Allow cookies for uaply.net (in Brave: the lion icon in the address bar, then set cookies to "Allow all" for this site, or switch Shields off for uaply.net) and reload the page.
  • A privacy request (a copy of your data, a correction): email us from the address in your profile. The Privacy Policy explains your rights.
