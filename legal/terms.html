3. What happens when you swipe right

Applications on your behalf. A swipe right is your instruction to apply, and you authorize UAply to complete and submit that application on your behalf using the information you provide. Depending on the listing, UAply:

  • sends it into the employer's hiring system, when the employer connected that system to UAply, posted the job on UAply, or uses a hiring system that takes applications from job boards directly (such as Recruitee's careers sites);
  • emails it, with your resume, to the address the employer publishes for applications;
  • fills in the employer's own application form and presses its Submit button for you, but only on a hiring system that shows no security check and whose terms do not forbid applying through someone else, and only if you have turned on Automatic submit (the separate tick in the box before your first swipe); or
  • everywhere else, fills in the employer's form in the UAply app and leaves it for you to review and submit yourself.

UAply never answers voluntary self-identification questions (such as race, gender, veteran status or disability), never completes security checks such as CAPTCHAs, and tells you an application was sent only when the employer confirms it, or, for an emailed application, when UAply's email service accepts it for delivery; UAply cannot see whether the employer read it. UAply adds no cover letter of its own; the optional Message to the employer note is sent only if you turn it on. You are responsible for the accuracy of your profile and of every application sent with it.

You can stop UAply sending applications for you at any time: turn off Send applications for me in Profile, Account. Swipes right then only save the job to Job Tracker.

Some sends include a short countdown during which you can press Undo; after it ends, an application cannot be recalled. Once an employer receives your details, their own privacy practices apply.
