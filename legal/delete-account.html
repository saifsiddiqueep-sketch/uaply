Delete your UAply account

Last updated October 22, 2026. This page is for UAply on the web (uaply.net) and the UAply app for Android and iPhone: it is the same account everywhere.

Delete it yourself (about a minute)

  1. Open UAply (the app, or uaply.net in any browser) and sign in to the account you want to delete. You do not need the phone app: the website works the same way.
  2. Tap Profile, then Account.
  3. Open More account options at the bottom, then tap Delete my account…
  4. A few optional questions about why you are leaving appear. Answer any and tap Send and continue, or tap Skip survey; deleting does not depend on them.
  5. Confirm: enter your password (or, if your account has no password, for example one made with Continue with Google, type DELETE), tick I understand this cannot be undone, and tap Delete my account.

The account is deleted at once. If you had a paid plan, it is cancelled with Stripe first, so your card is never charged again; if Stripe cannot be reached, the account is kept and the screen tells you, so you are never left paying for an account that no longer exists.

If you cannot sign in

Use Forgot password? on the Sign in screen first. If that does not work, email uaplytechnologies@uaply.net from the email address of the account and ask us to delete it. [privacy email] We may ask a question to confirm the account is yours, and we delete it within 30 days (usually within 2 business days).

What is deleted

  • From our server, at once: your account (email, password hash, the Google account number if you used Google), your sign-in sessions, your plan, the receipts we emailed you, the application records made while you were signed in, the help messages you sent while signed in, and any email for your account still waiting to be sent.
  • On your device: your profile, swipes and saved applications live in your browser or the app on that device. Remove them with Profile, Data & privacy, Delete all my data (or by uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's data for uaply.net).
  • Applications made while signed out are not linked to any account; email us with the job and the email address you used and we delete them too.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Backups: We keep only the four most recent backups, made before releases and restores. A deleted account disappears from them as they are replaced. If you want it removed from backups sooner, say so in your email and we will do it by hand.
  • Payments: if you ever paid, Stripe (our payment processor) keeps its own record of the payment, as tax and anti-fraud law requires. UAply never stores your card number.
  • Applications already sent: an application you sent is held by that employer under its own privacy policy; ask the employer to delete it.
  • Help requests that reached our inbox: a message sent with Talk to a person, or emailed to us, stays in the team's mailbox. Say so in your email and we delete it.
  • Addresses that bounced: if our email to you bounced or was reported as spam, your address stays on our do-not-send list for up to a year, so we never email it by mistake.
  • Your answers to the optional questions when you left are anonymous: they are not linked to your account.
