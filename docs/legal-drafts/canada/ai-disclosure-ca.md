## Screen copy

Some answers in your application may be drafted by our AI provider. You can read and change them before they are sent.

If we are not sure of an answer, or a question is sensitive, we do not guess. It goes to **Needs you**. We never send those without you.

The match percentage is worked out by a fixed formula. It never rejects you. Employers make every hiring decision.

## Policy paragraph

UAply uses an AI service, run by a company we call "our AI provider", to draft some application answers and to answer help questions. The text sent to it can include parts of your profile and the employer's question. We use it only to write the reply. You can review drafted answers before sending. Questions we are unsure about, or that are sensitive, are held under "Needs you" and are never sent without you. The AI provider is in the United States; see privacy-ca.md section 9.
