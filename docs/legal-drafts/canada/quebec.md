# Quebec: why UAply is "not available yet"

Quebec has extra rules. We have not built for them. Until counsel signs off, UAply is not offered in Quebec. This list is our understanding, for counsel to confirm and correct.

## What Quebec would need

**Privacy (Law 25)**
- A privacy officer, with title and contact published on our site. [TO FILL: name]
- A privacy impact assessment before we transfer personal information outside Quebec (we store it in the United States).
- Confidentiality-by-default settings: the most private choice is the starting choice.
- A notice when a decision is made exclusively by automated processing, and a right to have it reviewed by a person.
- Consent in clear, separate terms, in simple language, not buried in the terms.
- An incident register, and notice of serious incidents to the regulator (Commission d'accès à l'information) and affected people.

**Language (Charter of the French Language, Bill 96)**
- French versions of the terms, privacy policy, consents and contracts of adhesion.
- The app screens and support in French.
- Counsel to say if French must come first or be shown with equal prominence.
