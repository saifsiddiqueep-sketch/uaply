# UAply Privacy Policy (Canada)

Drafted to follow the 10 fair-information principles in PIPEDA. Company: [TO FILL: legal name]. "UAply", "we" means that company.

## 1. Why we collect your information (purposes)

We collect and use your personal information for these purposes only:

1. **Run your account.** Sign you in and keep your records.
2. **Find and rank jobs.** Show listings and a match percentage.
3. **Apply for you.** When you swipe right, prepare and send your application to the employer.
4. **Prove what happened.** Keep a record of what was sent, and send receipts.
5. **Support.** Answer your questions and fix problems.
6. **Payments.** Give you the paid plan you chose.
7. **Security and legal.** Prevent abuse and meet legal duties.
8. **Marketing email, only if you opt in.** See marketing-email-ca.md.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising. We will not use it for a new purpose without asking you first.

## 11. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw consent at any time:
- Stop applying for you: Profile, Account, turn off "Send applications for me".
- Stop for one employer: see consent-apply-ca.md.
- Stop marketing email: unsubscribe link.
- Delete your account: Profile, Account, More account options.

Consequences: without consent to send applications, UAply only saves jobs to Job Tracker. Applications already received by an employer cannot be recalled, and that employer's own policy applies. Deleting your account removes your sign-in, receipts and linked records. Some records may stay where the law requires.
