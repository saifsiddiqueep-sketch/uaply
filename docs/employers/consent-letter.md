# Consent to receive applications through UAply

[Back to the employer kit](README.md)

*Draft for review by a lawyer before first use. Send it to the employer, and keep their signed copy or their reply email. UAply records the
date and the name of the person who agreed in the employer list (the `consent` field); without it, nothing is sent.*

---

**Employer:** ____________________ (legal name)
**Contact who agrees:** ____________________ (name, job title, email)
**Hiring system and account:** ____________________ (for example "Workable, subdomain acme", or "email to jobs@acme.example")
**Jobs covered:** all published jobs / only these: ____________________

1. **What you agree to.** You agree that UAply may send you applications from people who chose to apply to your jobs in the UAply app, through
   the channel named above, starting on the date below.

2. **What UAply sends.** For each application: the details listed on the page for your hiring system in UAply's employer kit
   (`docs/employers/`), the resume file, and answers to your posting's own questions where the person's profile has them. Each application
   comes from one person who reviewed what would be sent and agreed to it.

3. **What UAply never sends.** Gender, race or ethnicity, veteran status, disability status, or any other voluntary self-identification.
   UAply does not answer a question it cannot answer from the person's own profile or their own words.

4. **Your key.** If you give UAply a key or token, you create it yourself, with only the rights your page in the kit names. UAply keeps it on
   its server only, never shows it, never writes it to a log, and uses it only to read your job's application questions and submit
   applications to your published jobs. UAply's test mode never uses it. UAply does not use your key to read, change or delete anything else.

5. **Your side.** You treat each application like one received on your own careers page. Hiring decisions are yours. Once an application
   reaches your system, you hold that person's data under your own privacy notice and the law that applies to you.

6. **Stopping.** You can stop at any time, without notice, by revoking the key (the steps are on your page in the kit) or by telling UAply at
   the address below. UAply then deletes its copy of your key and removes you from its employer list within 2 business days. Applications
   already delivered stay with you.

7. **Problems.** If an application looks wrong, or arrives where it should not, tell UAply at once. UAply stops sending until it is fixed.

8. **No fee.** Neither side pays the other under this letter.

UAply contact: ____________________ (support address)
