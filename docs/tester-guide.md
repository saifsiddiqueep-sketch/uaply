# UAply: a guide for testers

Thank you for trying UAply. It shows you one job at a time: swipe right (or tap Apply) on a job you want, swipe left (or tap Pass)
on one you don't. This page tells you how to start, what a swipe right really does, and how to tell us what you think.
Please use your own real details: applications can go to real employers.

## 1. Create your account

You need an account to use UAply.

1. Open UAply and go to the sign-up card.
2. Enter your email, your first and last name, and a password.
3. Tick the box to confirm you are 18 or older and agree to the Terms and the Privacy Policy, then create the account.
4. We email you a 6-digit code. Type it in to confirm the address is yours. The code works for 10 minutes, and only the newest one
   works; if it does not arrive, check your spam folder, then use Resend code.

You can also use Continue with Google. Next time, sign in with your email and password, with Google, or with a code we email you.

## 2. Set up your profile

Open **Profile**, then **Profile setup**, and fill in the steps. Adding your resume fills in much of it for you. UAply needs at
least your name, an email employers can reply to, and a phone number or a resume before a swipe right can apply; until then it
tells you what is missing. The match score on each card compares the job with your profile. It only orders the jobs for you: it
never rejects you and never makes a hiring decision.

The first time you swipe, UAply explains what an application sends and asks you to agree.

## 3. What a swipe right does

The card says which of these it will be:

- **Applies instantly.** The employer is connected to UAply. Your application (your profile details and your resume) goes straight
  to them, and you get an email receipt.
- **Ready.** The employer takes applications on its own site. Nothing is sent for you yet: the job is kept in Job Tracker as
  *Finish on their site*. From there, in the UAply phone app, UAply opens the employer's own application page and fills in what it
  can from your profile; you check it and press the employer's Submit button yourself. On the website, UAply opens the employer's
  page and copies your name, email, phone and links so you can paste them into their form.
- **Ready, one tap on their site.** The same, on a page that asks a quick "are you human" check. You do that check yourself; UAply
  never does it for you.

Questions about race, gender, veteran status or disability are never answered for you. You can tap **Undo** right after a swipe.
A swipe left only hides the job: nothing is sent, and Undo brings it back.

## 4. Job Tracker

**Job Tracker** is in the bar at the bottom of the screen. **Applied** lists every job you swiped right on, with its status. For a
*Finish on their site* job, tap **Finish applying on their site**, and when you have sent it, tap **Mark as applied**. You can move an
application to Interview, Accepted or Rejected as things happen.

## 5. Tell us what you think

Tap **Help** (the button at the bottom right, just above the bar) and choose:

- **Give feedback:** a star rating and a few words.
- **Report a problem:** what happened and what you were trying to do.
- **Talk to a person:** a question for the team, with the email we should reply to.

What reaches us: your rating and words, which screen you were on, your account (you are signed in, so we can look into what you
saw), and your email for a reply only if you give it. A problem report or a question is also emailed to the team at once. After you send it, the assistant says so: "Thank you! A person
on the UAply team reads every message." for feedback, and for a problem report or a question, "Sent to the UAply team. They will
reply to you by email." (or that they cannot reply if you left no email). A person reads every message. Please do not put
passwords or other private details in a message.

## 6. The three things we most want you to try

1. **Set up and swipe.** Create your account, add your resume, and swipe through at least 15 jobs, both directions. Does the match
   score make sense for you? Was anything confusing?
2. **Finish one application on an employer's site.** Swipe right on a *Ready* job you really want, then finish it from Job Tracker
   and tap Mark as applied. Tell us where it was hard.
3. **Report one thing.** Send us at least one piece of feedback or one problem report from Help, even a small one.

Thank you. Everything you tell us goes straight to the people building UAply.

## 7. If you are testing the phone app

A few things only a real phone can show. Please tell us (Help, then Report a problem) if any of them does not happen as written.

- **Back button (Android).** Open Job Tracker, then Profile, then press your phone's Back button: it goes to Job Tracker, then to
  the jobs. With a menu or a box open, Back closes it first. On the jobs, the first press says "Press Back again to leave UAply";
  only a second press within 2 seconds closes the app. This must be confirmed on a real Android phone: our computer tests only
  imitate the phone, so if the first Back press closes UAply at once, please report it.
- **Export buttons.** Profile > Data & privacy > Download backup (JSON) and Applications (CSV), and Add to calendar on an interview
  in Job Tracker: your phone's share sheet opens with the file, so you can save it to Files or Drive, or send it. If you close the
  share sheet, nothing happens and nothing is saved. If the app instead says "Copied ...", your app version cannot save files yet:
  paste the text somewhere to keep it. Profile > Data & privacy > Delete all my data also deletes these files from the app.
- **Turning the phone (iPhone).** The iPhone app stays upright when you turn the phone sideways.
- **Backups (Android).** What UAply keeps on your phone (your profile and swipes) is not part of the phone's automatic Google backup.
  Before you reinstall the app or switch phones, use Download backup (JSON) and keep the file; Restore on the same screen brings it back.
