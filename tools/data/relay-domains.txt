# Privacy relay domains: ALWAYS allowed to make a UAply account, checked before the throwaway list (auth_api.is_relay).
#
# Why: a relay gives the person a private address that forwards to their real, long-lived inbox, so applications, receipts and employers'
# replies still reach them. Sign in with Apple hands out privaterelay.appleid.com addresses; refusing relays would refuse those people.
# A domain under one of these (alias.anonaddy.com) counts too.
#
# How to edit: one domain per line, lower case, no "@". "#" starts a comment. Read by auth_api.relay_domains(); edits apply without a restart.
# Last reviewed: October 17, 2026.
